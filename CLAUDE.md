# jamesstew.art

James's personal site. Plain HTML and CSS, generated by `build.mjs` (zero dependencies; `vendor/marked.esm.js` is a frozen copy of marked). `node build.mjs serve` previews at localhost:8000.

## Commit messages

The commit history is public, and it's James's. Messages should read like he typed them himself: short, tactical, plain, lowercase is fine, and a light touch of personality at most. Example: `took the website out of the stone age - future proofing and making it not suck`.

- Never robotic or LLM-sounding. No conventional-commit prefixes (`feat:`, `chore:`), no sentence-case formality, no bulleted changelogs, no corporate verbs ("enhance," "streamline," "leverage"), and no over-the-top jokes.
- Every message can be different. Don't follow a template.
- **James is the sole author.** Never add a `Co-Authored-By` trailer or any other Claude attribution to commits or PRs in this repo.
- Shape: a short lowercase title line, plus a few terse sub-bullets when the change is big enough to need them.
- No approval round needed. Write it in his voice and commit.

## House rules

- **URLs never change.** Essays live at `/notes/<slug>/`, and the slug is the file name in `content/notes/`. Old `.html` URLs redirect via the `LEGACY` map in `build.mjs`.
- `/telluride-splitwise/`, `/mock-interview-machine/` and `/stablecoin-payouts-poc/` belong to other repos (GitHub Pages project sites). Never create those paths here.
- **Essay dates are intentional.** `date` is the date shown on the essay; don't "correct" it from git history.
- Facts about James (role, companies, projects) live in `content/site.json`. Don't invent biography; ask.
- Publishing: write the essay on the site first, then cross-post to LinkedIn with a link back.
- The Man in the Arena quote stays in the homepage source, as an HTML comment.
